Pin Oak Charity Horse Show — The Gold Standard

The Pin Oak Charity Horse Show in Houston, Texas is one of the most celebrated and beloved charity horse shows in North America — a prestigious hunter jumper competition that has raised millions of dollars for Texas Children's Hospital over its decades of history. Pin Oak represents the best of what a charity horse show can be: world-class competition combined with genuine philanthropic impact. The Pin Oak model has inspired countless regional charity shows across the country, making charity showing one of the most meaningful traditions in the hunter jumper community.

Why Charity Shows Matter to the Hunter Jumper Community

Charity horse shows create a unique opportunity for the equestrian community to leverage its passion for the sport toward meaningful social impact. They bring riders, trainers, sponsors, and spectators together around shared values — competition, excellence, and generosity — in a way that strengthens the community while supporting important causes. Elite Equine Marketplace is proud to support the promotion of charity shows and equestrian fundraisers across the hunter jumper world.
